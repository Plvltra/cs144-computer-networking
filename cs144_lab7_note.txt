疑问:
Q.为什么需要三个线程?每个线程的作用是什么?
A.bidrectional(main线程): stdin/stdout<==>ByteStream<==>TCPSocketEndToEnd
  TCPMinnowSocket(_tcp_main线程): TCPSocketEndToEnd<==>NetworkInterfaceAdapter
  endtoend(network_thread线程): NetworkInterfaceAdapter<==>Internet
Q.server的NetworkInterfaceAdapter是如何确定destination ip是192.168.0.50, 并wrap ip的?
A.server listen_and_accept时TCPOverIPv4Adapter::unwrap_tcp_in_ip, 将client ip设为adapter的destination
Q.host序与大端序的转换问题?
A.serialize的时候将integer的host序列转换为网络大端序, string是单字节不做转换

ByteStream: 在bidirectional_stream_copy
TCPSender, TCPReceiver(in TCPPeer): 在TCPMinnowSocket中间


用例: client(192.168.0.50)从stdin写入到发送的流程
1.stdin——>outbound(ByteStream) (bidrectional rule1)
2.outbound——>TCPSocketEndToEnd的fd(LocalStreamSocket, 与_thread_data连通) (bidrectional rule2)
3.TCPSocketEndToEnd的fd(_thread_data)——>_tcp(TCPPeer)写入, 并且_datagram_adapter(NetworkInterfaceAdapter, 192.168.0.50)执行write (TCPMinnowSocket rule2)
4.NetworkInterfaceAdapter write到fd()的数据——>NetworkInterfaceAdapter的frame_fd()
5.TCPSocketEndToEnd的NetworkInterfaceAdapter的frame_fd()——>router的interface(host_side, 192.168.0.1) recv_frame (network_thread: host to router)
6.调用route,从NetworkInterface(10.0.0.192)路由出(TCP header dst_ip由sock connect设置到NetworkAdapter的config里面) (network_thread: host to router)
7.NetworkInterface(10.0.0.192)的router_to_internet.write——>internet_socket.write (network_thread: router to Internet)
